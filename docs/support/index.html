Help

Getting started

Is Offizin really free, including for businesses?

Yes. Offizin is open source under the MIT License, which allows personal, commercial, nonprofit, government, and enterprise use at no cost. See the terms.

Do I need an account?

No. You need Microsoft Word or Google Docs, and that is all. Offizin has no sign-up and no server.

How do I make my first publication?

Open Offizin, enter your details on the Info tab, pick colors and fonts on the Design tab, then choose a publication on the Create tab and select Create publication. Replace the sample text and pictures, then use Check to find anything you missed.

What is the old German text in the templates?

It is filler, the way Word and other programs use the Latin "lorem ipsum." The lines come from Middle High German poems written about 800 years ago, such as the Nibelungenlied and the songs of Walther von der Vogelweide, so nobody mistakes them for finished text. Replace them with your own words. Names, dates, prices, phone numbers, emails, and addresses use plain placeholders instead, because their format matters. The Design Checker on the Check tab finds any filler you missed.

Why are the publications black and white?

Ink, a black and white scheme, is the default because it prints well on any printer and copies cleanly. Pick another scheme, or your own colors, on the Design tab at any time, and use Apply this design to the publication to change one you already made.

Can I download Offizin as a package?

Yes. The Word page and the Google Docs page each offer a package (a .zip file) with the install files and a plain text guide, which is handy for sharing or for an IT team. Unzip it first: Word's Upload My Add-in box and the Microsoft 365 admin center take the manifest file inside. Google Docs has no upload box for add-ins outside its Marketplace, so its three files go into a document's Apps Script editor, or IT teams can upload them with Google's clasp tool.

How do I replace a picture placeholder?

In Word, right-click the placeholder and choose Change Picture. In Google Docs, select it, then choose Replace image in the toolbar (or right-click it). The new picture keeps the placeholder's size and place.

Can I change colors or fonts after I make a publication?

Yes. Pick a new scheme on the Design tab and select Apply this design to the publication. Offizin switches every color and font in the publication exactly, and your text stays. Pictures keep their own colors. If you change your mind, Offizin's Undo puts the previous colors and fonts back.

Can I adjust the padding, margins, and spacing?

Yes. On the Create tab, open a publication and expand Customize. You can change the paper size, orientation, and margins, and use the sliders for padding inside panels and boxes, the gaps between columns, text size, line spacing, and the space between paragraphs. The preview updates as you go. To make your choices the starting point for every publication, set them under Spacing and text on the Design tab.

How does Offizin's Undo work?

Offizin keeps its own history of what it did: creating a publication, inserting page parts, applying a design, and changing page setup. Offizin's Undo and Redo reverse only those actions and leave your own typing and formatting alone, so you can still use Word's or Google Docs' own Undo for everything else. Undoing a page part removes it, and Redo puts it back with whatever you typed into it. Undoing a design puts the previous colors and fonts back. Undoing a new publication restores the document as it was before, so Offizin asks first. The history lasts while the Offizin panel stays open; in Google Docs, a new publication can be undone for up to six hours.

How do I print one label, badge, or certificate per person?

Open a publication that offers Mail merge (address labels, name badges, place cards, certificates, envelopes, and tickets), then paste your list or open a .csv file. Copy the rows straight from Excel or Google Sheets, include the heading row, and Offizin matches the columns to the right places. It makes as many sheets as your list needs, up to 1,000 people.

What does Finish publication do?

Offizin marks the parts it inserts with hidden markers, which show nothing on the page and add no frame, so its Undo can find them. When your publication is done, select Finish publication on the Check tab to remove the markers. Your content stays exactly as it is.

Printing

How do I print a brochure on both sides?

Print side 1, put the paper back in the printer, and print side 2. With automatic two-sided printing, choose flip on short edge for landscape publications. The Check tab has folding diagrams for each publication type.

Why does my printer leave a white border?

Most home printers cannot print to the very edge of the paper, so Offizin keeps content inside a safe margin. For color that runs off the edge, send a PDF to a print shop and ask about bleed.

My business cards or labels do not line up.

Print at actual size (100%), with any "fit to page" option turned off. Print a test on plain paper and hold it over the card or label sheet against a window. If everything is off by the same amount, adjust the page margins in Word's Layout tab or Google Docs' Page setup.

Can Offizin set my printer settings?

Word and Google Docs do not let add-ins open or change the print dialog. Offizin does two things instead. First, where Word allows it, Offizin saves the settings Word keeps inside the document: book fold for booklets, mirror margins, and two pages per sheet for small pages. Word allows it in Microsoft 365 Word version 2507 or later on Windows and 16.99.2 or later on Mac, and in a file made with Open in a new document, which can also keep its fonts, which helps print shops. In Word on the web, in Word 2019, 2021, and 2024, and in older Microsoft 365 versions, Replace this document cannot set them, so set them yourself in the Word app: choose Layout, Margins, Custom Margins, then under Multiple pages pick Book fold, Mirror margins, or 2 pages per sheet. For a binding edge, make the inside margin wider under Customize. Second, the Check tab lists the exact choices to make in the print dialog for your publication, such as the paper size, two-sided printing with the right flip edge, and actual size.

How do I print a booklet?

In Microsoft 365 Word version 2507 or later on Windows and 16.99.2 or later on Mac, and in a booklet made with Open in a new document, book fold is already on, so Word arranges the pages on each sheet for you. Print on both sides, flipping on the short edge, then fold the stack in half and staple on the fold. In Word on the web, in Word 2019, 2021, and 2024, and in older Microsoft 365 versions, Replace this document makes separate half-size pages with no book fold, and opening that document in the Word app does not add it. Either turn book fold on yourself in the Word app (Layout, Margins, Custom Margins, then Multiple pages, Book fold, and set the paper size to Letter or A4, because the pages are already half size), or use your printer's booklet setting, which arranges the pages and chooses the flip edge for you. Google Docs cannot arrange booklet pages, so Offizin lays out the printer spreads itself: print the pages in order on both sides, flipping on the short edge.

How do I make a PDF?

In Word, choose File, then Save As or Export, and pick PDF. In Google Docs, choose File, Download, then PDF document.

Resumes and CVs

Which resume should I use for online applications?

Use one of the plain resumes. Many employers run applications through screening software (applicant tracking systems), which reads simple, single column documents most reliably. The plain resumes use one column, standard section headings, ordinary bullets, and no tables, lines, text boxes, or pictures, and they keep your contact details in the body of the page, where the software looks. Save the designed resume for printing and handing out in person.

What is the difference between a resume and a CV?

In the United States and Canada, a resume is a short summary of your experience, usually one or two pages, and a curriculum vitae (CV) is a longer academic record with publications, grants, teaching, and presentations. In the UK and much of Europe, "CV" is the everyday word for a resume. Offizin has a plain academic CV and a UK and Europe style CV.

Word

The page size did not change in Word on the web.

Publications from the Create tab bring their own page size. If yours did not change, choose Layout, then Size, then More Paper Sizes, and enter the size Offizin showed. Word on the web does not yet let add-ins change page size directly, so the Page setup buttons appear only in Word for Windows and Mac.

I do not see Upload My Add-in.

Your organization has turned off personal add-in uploads. Ask your IT team to deploy Offizin from the Microsoft 365 admin center. The steps are on the Word page under Whole organization.

I used Layout Toolkit before. What changed?

Layout Toolkit is now Offizin. The old version still works where it is installed, but it no longer receives updates. Install Offizin from the Word page, then remove Layout Toolkit from My Add-ins.

Google Docs

Google says the app is not verified.

Because you pasted Offizin into your document yourself, it is your own private script and Google has not reviewed it. Select Advanced, then Go to Offizin (unsafe). If Google lists the two permissions with checkboxes, check both before you continue; Offizin needs both. Each copy of a document is its own script, so each copy asks the first time Offizin opens in it. Offizin can only change the document it is in. Details are on the Google Docs page.

The Offizin menu does not appear.

Reload the document. If it still does not appear, open Extensions, Apps Script, and check that the files are named exactly Code.gs and Sidebar.html, then save again.

My logo disappears in Google Docs.

Google's settings storage is too small for pictures, so Offizin keeps your logo in your browser. If your browser blocks storage for the sidebar, add the logo again each session, or keep it in a brand kit file on the Info tab.

My details and design are gone in a copy of the document.

Each copy of a document is its own Apps Script project, and Google keeps a script's settings separate, so a new copy starts with the default design and an empty Info tab. Save a brand kit once on the Info tab (Save brand kit) and open it in each new copy with Open brand kit. A private add-on published through the Marketplace SDK (below) is one script, so there your settings follow you from document to document.

How can a Google Workspace administrator publish Offizin for the whole organization?

Google allows private add-ons for one Workspace organization through the Google Workspace Marketplace SDK. In outline:

  1. Create a standalone Apps Script project (script.google.com) owned by an account in your organization, and add the three Offizin files.
  2. Link it to a standard Google Cloud project, configure the OAuth consent screen as Internal, and enable the Google Workspace Marketplace SDK.
  3. Create a versioned deployment, then in the Marketplace SDK fill in the app configuration (Docs add-on) and the store listing, and choose Private visibility.
  4. Publish, then install it for your domain from the Google Workspace Marketplace.

Google's guide: Publish an app to the Google Workspace Marketplace. A public Marketplace listing for Offizin is planned.
