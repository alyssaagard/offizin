Privacy policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Offizin does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information. It has no server, no accounts, no analytics, and no cookies.

What Offizin is

Offizin is an add-in for Microsoft Word and an Apps Script add-on for Google Docs, published by Alyssa I. Agard. Its program files are static web pages served from this website by GitHub Pages.

Your documents

Offizin builds publications inside Word or Google Docs on your device. The content of your documents is never sent to Offizin or to anyone else by Offizin.

Your settings

  • Word and the website demo: your color and font choices, business information, and logo are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. Offizin also saves a small note inside each publication it creates (the template name, design, and the sample text it used) so the design checker and restyling can work. That note stays in the document.
  • Google Docs: your settings and business information are saved with Google's Apps Script properties in your own Google account. Your logo is saved in your browser's local storage. A note like the one above is saved in the document's properties.
  • Undo history: so that Offizin's Undo can restore a document after you create a publication, Offizin keeps a copy of the document as it was just before. In Word and in the website demo, that copy stays in the Offizin panel's memory on your device and disappears when the panel closes. In Google Docs, it is kept in Google's temporary Apps Script cache for your document and is deleted automatically within six hours. It is never sent anywhere else.
  • Pasted mail merge lists are used only to build the publication on your device or in your own Google account. Offizin does not save them inside the document's properties.
  • You can delete your settings at any time by clearing your browser's site data, or, in Google Docs, by removing the script from your document.

Services that see your connection

  • GitHub Pages serves this website and Offizin's files. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts that the live demo and the Offizin panels use for previews. Your browser requests them from Google, as described in the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • Microsoft provides Office.js, the library every Word add-in loads, and runs Word itself. Google runs Google Docs and Apps Script. Their own privacy terms apply to your use of those products.

Children

Offizin collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. The history of every change is public in the source repository.

Contact
