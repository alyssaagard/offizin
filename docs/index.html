Desktop publishing for Word and Google Docs

Make it look printed, not typed.

Offizin puts a publishing studio inside the word processor you already use. Pick a brochure, newsletter, flyer, card, or calendar, and it arrives in your colors, your fonts, and your business details, ready to print and fold.

Try it in your browser Add to Word Add to Google Docs

Moving on from Microsoft Publisher? Publisher reached its end of life in October 2026. Offizin keeps the parts people loved: a gallery of publications, color and font schemes, business information sets, page parts, a design checker, and folding guides.

Dozens of publications, ready in seconds

Every template adapts to your paper, colors, fonts, and business information, and every one can be customized: paper size, margins, padding, spacing, and text size. Click one to open it in the live demo.

Everything Publisher users reach for

Publication gallery

Brochures, newsletters, booklets, flyers, posters, signs, business cards, postcards, greeting cards, invitations, programs, menus, letterhead, envelopes, labels, name badges, tickets, gift certificates, calendars, certificates, resumes, and CVs.

Color and font schemes

Black and white by default, with twelve more color schemes and nine font pairings, or your own colors and fonts. Change the scheme later and every color and font in the publication switches exactly, with your text kept.

Business information

Enter your name, organization, address, phone, website, and logo once. Every card, letterhead, and brochure fills them in. Keep separate sets for work, a club, or home. In Google Docs, save a brand kit once and open it in each new document.

Page parts

Drop in pull quotes, sidebars, coupons, attention getters, event details, schedules, contact blocks, and testimonials, all styled to match.

Design checker

Finds sample text you forgot to replace, picture placeholders still waiting for photos, and business details you never filled in. One click takes you to each one.

Print and fold guides

Fold lines on screen, never on paper. Booklet printing, mirror margins, and two pages per sheet are saved in Word documents where Word allows it (Microsoft 365 Word 2507 or later on Windows, 16.99.2 or later on Mac, or a file made with Open in a new document), and every publication shows the exact print settings to choose.

Mail merge

Paste a list from Excel or Google Sheets and print one address label, name badge, certificate, envelope, or ticket per person, on as many sheets as it takes.

Customize everything

Paper size, orientation, margins, padding inside panels, gaps between columns, text size, line spacing, and paragraph spacing, with a live preview. Set your own defaults once.

Its own Undo

Offizin keeps a history of what it did, so you can undo a page part or a new design without losing the typing you did in Word or Google Docs.

Resumes screening software can read

Plain, single column resumes with standard headings and no tables or text boxes, for online applications, plus academic and European style CVs.

Native to your editor

Real Title and Heading styles, real bullets, and real flowing columns in Word, so the Styles pane, Navigation pane, and screen readers understand every publication.

What Offizin does differently from Publisher

Offizin builds publications from styles, tables, and pictures that Word and Google Docs understand everywhere, including on the web. That keeps documents easy to edit, share, and print. A few things work differently:

  • Panels are fixed table cells rather than free-floating text boxes, so they never drift when someone edits the text.
  • In Word, text flows between columns natively. Google Docs balances columns when the publication is made, because it cannot flow text through add-ons.
  • Your work stays a normal Word or Google document, so anyone can open and edit it without Offizin.
